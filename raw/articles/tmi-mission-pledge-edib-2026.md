# THE TMI MISSION PLEDGE

I begin this journey not merely to learn about finance, but to answer a call from my soul. I recognize that my wealth, my time, and my knowledge are not my own; they are an Amanah — a sacred trust from Allah Subhanahu wa Ta'ala. My goal is not just profit, my goal is purity.

***My ultimate metric is not the return on my investment, but the return on my Akhirah.***

*Therefore, with Allah as my witness, I make this niyyah (intention) and pledge to:*

## Uphold the Primacy of the Akhirah

I will strive to make every financial decision through the lens of the Day of Judgment, seeking Hasanat (good deeds) over worldly gains and prioritizing the pleasure of Allah above all else.

## Pursue Purity with Unshakeable Integrity

I will be relentless in my effort to purify my wealth from Riba (interest) and all Haram sources. I will not compromise my principles for convenience or profit.

## Transform Knowledge into Action (Amal)

I will not be a passive student. I will take the knowledge I gain on this platform and actively apply it to my life, taking responsibility for its stewardship.

## View My Wealth as a Tool for Khayr (Good)

I will see my portfolio not as an end in itself, but as an engine to fuel my 'Ibadah (worship), to increase my Sadaqah (charity), and to become a pillar of strength for my family and the Ummah.

## Embrace the Journey with Sabr (Patience) and Tawakkul (Trust)

I will be patient during trials, grateful during blessings, and trust in Allah's plan, knowing that true success lies only with Him.

***This is my pledge. This is my mission. May Allah accept it from me and make me a source of benefit*** ***for the Ummah. Ameen.***
