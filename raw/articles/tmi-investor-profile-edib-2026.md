## PART 7: ISLAMIC GUIDANCE FOR YOUR LEVEL

Given that your Knowledge Level is **Building**, I want to give you the two most essential Islamic finance foundations that every serious Muslim investor must understand before anything else.

**First: The Prohibition of Riba — Understanding What You Are Avoiding.** Riba is not just "interest" in the narrow sense. It encompasses any predetermined, guaranteed return on money lent or invested — detached from real economic activity or risk-sharing. Allah ﷻ says: "Allah has permitted trade and has forbidden interest." (Quran 2:275). This is not a minor preference — this is a boundary defined directly by Allah ﷻ. The practical implication for you as an investor in Germany is that conventional savings accounts, government bonds, corporate bonds, and most insurance products contain Riba by structure. Your first task is identifying where Riba exists in your current financial life — and your Course 1 Purification Audit is the tool for this.

**Second: AAOIFI Shariah Standards — Your Compliance Framework.** When we say an investment is "Shariah-compliant" at TMI, we mean it meets the standards set by **AAOIFI — the Accounting and Auditing Organization for Islamic Financial Institutions**, which is the globally recognized institutional body for Islamic finance standards. For equities, AAOIFI screening involves two layers: qualitative screening (the company's core business must be permissible — no alcohol, weapons, conventional finance, entertainment of impermissible nature) and quantitative financial ratio screening (limits on interest-bearing debt, interest income, and impermissible cash ratios). Understanding AAOIFI is not academic — it is your practical tool for ensuring every euro you invest is halal.
