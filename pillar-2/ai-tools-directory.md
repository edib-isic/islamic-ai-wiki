## Halal Considerations

Most AI tools are halal-friendly since they provide software services. However:

- **Avoid:** Tools that integrate with haram platforms (gambling, adult content)
- **Check:** Payment processors don't embed interest-based products
- **Be aware:** Some tools use AI-generated content that might contain impermissible elements (review output)
