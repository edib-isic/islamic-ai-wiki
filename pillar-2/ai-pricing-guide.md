# Pricing Guide for AI Services

Price too low, you attract bad clients. Price too high, you scare away good ones. The sweet spot: charge what reflects your value, but stay competitive while building your reputation.

## Pricing Principles

1. **Value-based, not time-based** — Price on the outcome, not hours worked
2. **Clear scope** — Define deliverables upfront to avoid scope creep
3. **Raise prices** — Every 3 happy clients, increase 20-30%
4. **Packages > hourly** — Packages let clients see the value clearly

## Service Pricing Tiers

### AI Automation Setup

| Package | Price | What's Included |
|---------|-------|-----------------|
| Basic | €300-500 | 1 automation workflow, setup, training |
| Standard | €800-1,500 | 3-5 workflows, integration, 1 month support |
| Premium | €2,000-5,000 | Full automation system, custom tools, 3 months support |

### AI Chatbots

| Package | Price | What's Included |
|---------|-------|-----------------|
| Basic | €500 | Simple Q&A bot, website integration |
| Standard | €1,500 | Multi-language, knowledge base, analytics |
| Premium | €3,000-5,000 | Custom AI model, CRM integration, ongoing optimization |

### AI Content Systems

| Package | Price | What's Included |
|---------|-------|-----------------|
| Basic | €400/month | 10-15 blog posts/month, SEO optimized |
| Standard | €800/month | 20-30 posts, social media, email content |
| Premium | €1,500-3,000/month | Full content engine, multiple channels, A/B testing |

### Consulting & Strategy

| Engagement | Price | What's Included |
|------------|-------|-----------------|
| Audit | €500-1,000 | Business AI readiness assessment, roadmap |
| Hourly | €100-200/hour | Expert advice, troubleshooting |
| Retainer | €1,000-3,000/month | Ongoing strategy and guidance |

## When to Charge Hourly

- Discovery projects (scope unclear)
- Consulting/advice sessions
- Maintenance/ongoing support
- Quick fixes

**Rate:** Start at €50-80/hour, move to €100-150/hour after 10 clients.

## When to Charge Fixed Price

- Clear scope and deliverables
- Well-defined project
- You can estimate effort accurately

**Rule:** Fixed price = estimated hours × hourly rate × 1.5 (buffer for unknowns)

## Payment Terms (Halal)

- **Upfront:** 50% deposit, 50% on completion
- **Milestones:** For larger projects, split into 3 payments
- **Retainers:** Monthly, paid in advance
- **Late payments:** Define late fee in contract (fixed amount, not interest-based)

## Red Flags in Pricing

- Client wants everything cheap — they'll be difficult
- Scope unclear before quoting — define it first
- "Can you do this for free as a test?" — offer paid pilot instead
- Payment terms longer than 30 days — risk of non-payment

## Raise Prices Script

> "I've been working with more clients and the demand has exceeded capacity. Starting [date], my rates will be adjusted to reflect the increased value I'm delivering. For existing clients, I'll honor current rates for [timeframe]."
