# AI Agency Blueprint — Build a Halal AI Business

An AI agency sells AI-powered services to businesses. You deliver outcomes, not just code. This is how you scale from freelancer to business owner.

## Why AI Agency Works Right Now

- Businesses want AI but don't know where to start
- You have technical skills (software engineer)
- AI tools let one person deliver what used to take a team
- High demand, low supply of quality providers

## Business Structure (Halal)

### Legal structure options (Germany)

| Structure | Setup Cost | Liability | Tax Notes |
|-----------|-----------|-----------|-----------|
| Freelancer (Selbstständig) | €0 | Personal | Simpler, but full liability |
| UG (haftungsbeschränkt) | €1 minimum | Limited | Good starting point |
| GmbH | €25,000 minimum | Limited | Standard for established business |

**Recommendation:** Start as freelancer, move to UG at €5K/month revenue.

### Bank + Payments (Riba-free)

- **Banking:** Use Islamic bank or conventional without interest-bearing accounts
- **Payments:** Stripe/PayPal (standard), but avoid interest-bearing balances
- **Contracts:** Clear, fixed-price or hourly — no ambiguity

## Service Offerings

### Tier 1: Done-For-You (Highest margin)

Build and run AI systems for clients:
- AI content systems (blogs, social media, emails)
- AI customer support bots
- AI lead generation workflows
- AI data processing pipelines

**Price:** €1,000-5,000 per project + €300-1,000/month maintenance

### Tier 2: Consulting (Expertise)

Advise businesses on AI strategy:
- AI readiness assessment
- Process automation recommendations
- Team training on AI tools

**Price:** €100-200/hour or €2,000-5,000 per engagement

### Tier 3: Products (Scalable)

Build reusable tools/templates:
- AI workflow templates (Zapier/Make)
- AI-powered SaaS tools
- Training courses

**Price:** €50-500 per sale, passive income

## Operations

### Solo (Month 1-6)

You do everything:
- Sales
- Delivery
- Customer service

Target: €3,000-5,000/month revenue

### With Subcontractors (Month 6-12)

Hire freelancers for delivery:
- You handle sales + strategy
- Subcontractors do the work
- You keep 30-50% margin

Target: €5,000-15,000/month revenue

### Full Agency (Year 2+)

Team structure:
- Sales lead
- Delivery team (3-5 developers)
- Customer success
- You: CEO/strategy

Target: €15,000-50,000+/month revenue

## Getting Clients

### Organic (Free)

1. **LinkedIn** — Post AI content daily, connect with business owners
2. **Content marketing** — Blog/videos about AI automation
3. **Referrals** — Happy clients refer others

### Paid (Scale)

1. **Google Ads** — Target "AI automation" keywords
2. **Facebook/Instagram** — Target business owners
3. **Cold email** — Personalized outreach at scale

### Muslim Community Advantage

Target Muslim-owned businesses:
- They value halal services
- They trust someone from the community
- Underserved market, less competition

## Financial Management

### Track these numbers

- **Revenue** (monthly)
- **Expenses** (tools, subcontractors, ads)
- **Profit** (revenue - expenses)
- **Client acquisition cost** (ads spent / new clients)
- **Lifetime value** (average revenue per client over time)

### Allocate profit (Islamic approach)

- **50-70%** — Reinvest in business (growth)
- **20-40%** — Personal income
- **5-10%** — Zakat/Sadaqah (ongoing charity)

This allocation keeps you aligned with [Sadaqah Jariyah](../concepts/sadaqah-jariyah.md) goals while growing sustainably.

## Tools You'll Use

- **Sales:** LinkedIn, Cold email tools, CRM
- **Delivery:** AI coding tools, automation platforms, LLM APIs
- **Management:** Notion, Slack, project management tools
- **Finance:** Accounting software, invoicing tools

## Halal Guardrails

- No interest-based loans or financing
- No haram industry clients
- Transparent contracts (no hidden fees or deceptive pricing)
- Pay subcontractors fairly and on time
- Treat customers honestly (over-deliver, under-promise)
