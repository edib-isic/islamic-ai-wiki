# Riba — Prohibition of Interest

Riba encompasses any predetermined, guaranteed return on money lent or invested — detached from real economic activity or risk-sharing. Not merely "interest" in a narrow sense, but a structural prohibition on money generating money without underlying economic substance.

## Primary Sources

### Quran — The Severity

> "Allah has permitted trade and has forbidden interest." [Quran 2:275.md](Quran 2:275.md)

> "O you who have believed, fear Allah and give up what remains of interest, if you should be believers. And if you do not, then be informed of a war from Allah and His Messenger." [Quran 2:278-279.md](Quran 2:278-279.md)

The Quran declares war from Allah and His Messenger on those who continue Riba after prohibition. This is not a minor preference — it is a boundary defined directly by Allah.

### Hadith

> "Riba has 70 levels, the least of which is like a man committing fornication with his own mother."^[raw/articles/tmi-compass-iirs-edib-2026.md]

## Practical Implications

Conventional products containing Riba:
- Conventional savings accounts
- Government bonds
- Corporate bonds
- Most insurance products
- Interest-bearing loans/mortgages

For Muslim investors in any country, identifying and eliminating these is the first task.

## Wealth Purification

Uncertain halal status of wealth casts shadow on:
- Du'as made with it
- Zakat paid from it
- Sadaqah given with it

From [TMI Purification Audit](entities/tmi-the-muslim-investor.md): any impermissible elements in portfolio require immediate following of Emergency Purification Guide.^[raw/articles/tmi-investor-profile-edib-2026.md]

## Elimination Strategy

From [IIRS Compass Report](raw/articles/tmi-compass-iirs-edib-2026.md):

1. Identify all Riba exposure (debt, accounts, investments)
2. Prioritize by interest rate and balance
3. Allocate surplus cash flow to elimination
4. Track progress with IIRS score (target: 75+ for "Investment Ready")

Current status: ~$45,000 total debt, ~$4/day flowing to interest (~$115/month). Accelerated elimination path: ~28 months vs. ~5+ years on minimum payments.^[raw/articles/tmi-compass-iirs-edib-2026.md]

## Riba in AI Business Context

AI businesses must also avoid Riba:
- Do not hold business funds in interest-bearing accounts
- Use Riba-free financing options when needed
- Be aware of platforms that embed interest-based structures
