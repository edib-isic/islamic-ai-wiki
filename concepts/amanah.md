# Amanah — Sacred Trust

Amanah means a sacred trust from Allah. Wealth, time, and knowledge are not owned by the individual — they are entrusted. Every trustee (manager) will be audited on Judgment Day.

## Primary Sources

### Quran

> "Believe in Allah and His Messenger and spend from that of which He has made you trustees." [[Quran 57:7]]

> "And whatever you spend of good — it is for yourselves, and you do not spend except seeking the countenance of Allah." [[Quran 2:272]]

### Hadith

Five things asked on Judgment Day (Sunan al-Tirmidhi 2417):
1. How you lived your life
2. How you utilized your youth
3. With what means did you earn your wealth
4. How did you spend your wealth
5. What did you do with your knowledge

Items 3, 4, and 5 directly concern wealth and knowledge as Amanah.

## In Islamic Finance Context

- Every financial decision is an act of stewardship
- Portfolio is not "mine" — it is managed on behalf of the Owner
- Halal investing is not optional preference — it is fiduciary duty to the Trustor
- The audit is real; preparation matters now

## Practical Implications for AI Work

- AI tools and knowledge are Amanah — use them for halal purposes only
- Income generated through AI must follow [[concepts/riba.md|Riba-free]] principles
- Teaching AI skills to others is fulfilling the Amanah of knowledge
- Every business decision with AI should pass the Amanah test: would this be acceptable on Judgment Day?
