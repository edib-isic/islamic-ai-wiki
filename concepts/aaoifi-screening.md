# AAOIFI Shariah Standards

AAOIFI = Accounting and Auditing Organization for Islamic Financial Institutions. The globally recognized institutional body setting Shariah compliance standards for Islamic finance.

## Role in TMI Framework

When [TMI](entities/tmi-the-muslim-investor.md) says an investment is "Shariah-compliant," it means it meets AAOIFI standards. Understanding AAOIFI is described as not academic — it is the practical tool for ensuring every euro invested is halal.^[raw/articles/tmi-investor-profile-edib-2026.md]

## Equity Screening (Two Layers)

### 1. Qualitative Screening

Core business must be permissible. Impermissible businesses:
- Alcohol
- Weapons
- Conventional finance (interest-based)
- Entertainment of impermissible nature

### 2. Quantitative Financial Ratio Screening

Limits on:
- Interest-bearing debt
- Interest income
- Impermissible cash ratios

## Sukuk Screening

All Sukuk instruments must meet AAOIFI standards for:
- Structure legitimacy
- Underlying asset legitimacy

Sukuk = Islamic fixed income structured around real underlying assets (not bonds — profit from asset-backed arrangement, not interest).

## Practical Application for AI Businesses

AI businesses should also understand AAOIFI principles:
- Revenue must come from permissible activities
- Debt structures should avoid guaranteed interest returns
- When investing profits, use AAOIFI-screened vehicles
