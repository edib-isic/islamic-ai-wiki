Muhammad wasn't a myth. He wasn't an angel. He wasn't part god. He was a person who lived in seventh-century Arabia, ate, slept, felt pain, lost people, bled, and died. And the moment you understand that, everything else about Islam becomes clearer.

Allah said through him:

"Reci: 'Ja sam čovjek kao i Vi, — samo meni se objavljuje da je vaš Bog samo jedan Bog...'" (41:6)

Say: "I am a man like you, to whom it has been revealed that your God is only one God."

Not "I am divine." Not "I am above you." I am like you.

The book notes: "On je sin majke koja je jela meso na suncu sušeno." He is the son of a mother who ate sun-dried meat. A normal human mother. Nothing supernatural. No virgin birth. No miraculous origin story. Just a human being, chosen by Allah to carry a message.

And that message changed the world.

## His Character Before the Message

Before he was a prophet, people already knew him. His enemies knew him. His tribe knew him. And they gave him a name: *al-amin*. The trustworthy one.

The book records this moment. When the Qur'an commanded:

"I opominji rodbinu svoju najbližu" (26:214) — and warn your nearest kindred

Muhammad climbed the hill of Safa in Mecca and called his tribe. When they gathered, he asked them something direct:

"Predpostavimo da vam kažem kako se naoružana konjica okupila u dolini i hoće da vas napadne, — da li bi mi povjerovali?"

Suppose I tell you that an armed cavalry has gathered in the valley and is about to attack you — would you believe me?

They answered without hesitation: "Nikad nam nisi lagao." You never lied to us.

Then he said: "Ako je tako — onda vas opominjem i upozoravam da vam prijeti i predstoji teška kazna."

If that's so — then I warn you and caution you that a severe punishment is about to strike you.

His uncle Abdul-'Uzza shouted: "Nesrećniče jedan, zar si nas zbog toga sazvao?" You miserable man, is that why you called us?

But nobody denied his honesty. Nobody said, "he's lying now." Even after this, even with everything coming, they still knew: Muhammad doesn't lie.

The book records that his bitterest enemies couldn't dispute it: "Nije se ustanovilo da je Muhammed, posljednji Božiji poslanik, ikada slagao, niti je ikada povjerenje iznevjerio."

It was never established that Muhammad, the last Messenger of Allah, ever lied, or ever betrayed trust.

And even after everything — after his message destroyed their power, after they lost wealth and status and influence because of him — his greatest enemy, Abu Sufyan, once admitted:

"Nisam nikada vidio obljubljenijeg čovjeka među njegovim drugovima, kao Muhammeda."

I never saw a more beloved man among his companions than Muhammad.

That's not something you say about someone you hate. That's what you say about a man whose character you can't deny.

## The Life He Lived

He didn't grow up in comfort. He grew up knowing what it means to have nothing and lose everything.

His father died before he was born. His mother died when he was six years old. The Qur'an reminds him — and us:

"Lar nisi bio siroče, pa ti je On utočište pružio, i za pravu vjeru nisi znao, pa te je na pravi put uputio, i siromah si bio, pa te je imućnim učinio? Zato siroče ne ucvili, a na prosjaka ne podvikni i o blagodati Gospodara svoga kazuj!" (93:6)

Did He not find you an orphan and give you shelter? Did He not find you ignorant and guide you? Did He not find you in need and enrich you? Therefore, do not oppress the orphan, do not shout at the beggar, and speak of your Lord's favors.

He was raised by his grandfather Abdul-Muttalib, then by his uncle Abu Talib after his grandfather died. He worked from childhood. Herding camels. Doing manual labor. Later, he was hired by a wealthy Meccan widow named Khadijah to lead her trade caravans.

The book says: "Kroz takav život neposredno se upoznao sa životom bijednih i bespravnih i s njima dijelio dobro i zlo. Bio im je bliži nego kurejševićkoj aristokraciji."

Through that life, he became directly familiar with the lives of the poor and the oppressed and shared their good and bad with them. He was closer to them than to the Qurayshite aristocracy.

And that's why the attacks on him came from the top, not the bottom. The Qurayshite leaders didn't like what he was saying because his message threatened their power and privileges.

## What His Message Threatened

The Qur'an called out practices the Quraysh relied on. One of them: burying baby girls alive.

"Kada živa sahranjena djevojčica bude upita: zbog kakve krivice je umorena?"

When the buried-alive girl will be asked: for what sin was she killed?

The message told the tribal elders to give up their exploitative privileges. It told them society should offer equal chances to everyone. It told them the strong don't get to abuse the weak just because they're strong.

The book states: "Objava je išla za tim da plemenske starješine i oligarhija ublaže svoje privilegije koje su bile izrabljivačke, i da se, u društvenom smislu, katima pruže izjednačene šanse i uslovi."

The revelation aimed for the tribal elders and oligarchy to ease their exploitative privileges, and for society to offer equal chances and conditions to all.

But the rich and powerful couldn't tolerate those changes. They started persecuting Muslims, torturing them, forcing them to flee their homes. Some lost everything.

## The Offers They Made Him

They didn't just attack him. They tried to buy him.

A Qurayshite nobleman named Utba ibn Rabiah came to him, authorized by his people, and made an offer. The book records his exact words:

"Ti si, Muhammede, izuzetno cijenjen u svojoj sredini. Pozivom kojim si počeo zagovarati islam, napravio si veliki razdor i podvojenost u svom narodu. Doveo si u sumnju njihove dosadašnje ideale i tradiciju."

You are Muhammad, extremely respected among your people. With the call through which you began advocating Islam, you have created great division and split within your people. You have brought their ideals and traditions into question.

Then the offer:

"Bratiću moj! Ako to što činiš radiš zbog imetka i materijalne koristi, mi smo prikupili toliko imetka da ćeš biti najbogatiji među nama. Ako želiš položaj i vlast, dodjeljujemo ti je i ništa nećemo poduzeti niti uraditi bez tebe. Ako želiš da budeš naš vladar, ustoličićemo te. Ako misliš da je to što te opsjeda bolest koju ne možeš svojom voljom otkloniti, mi ćemo pokušati da te liječimo i potrošimo na tebe svu našu imovinu dok te ne izliječimo..."

Brother! If you do what you do for wealth and material gain, we have gathered so much wealth that you will be the richest among us. If you want position and power, we will grant it and will do nothing without you. If you want to be our ruler, we will crown you. If you think what troubles you is an illness you cannot cure by your own will, we will try to treat you and spend all our wealth on you until we heal you...

Muhammad asked: "Jesi li završio?" Have you finished?

"I sam," Utba replied.

Then Muhammad recited from the Qur'an:

"Hamim, Objava je od Milostivog, Samilosnog. Knjiga čiji su ajeti jasno izloženi, Kur'an na arapskom jeziku za ljude koji znaju, vjesnik radosnih vijesti i opomena..." (41:1-5)

Heim. Revelation is from the Compassionate, the Merciful. A Book whose verses are clearly laid out, the Qur'an in Arabic for people who know, bringing good tidings and warning...

And he continued until he reached:

"Ne padajte licem na tle pred Suncem ni pred Mjesecom, već padajte licem na tle pred Allahom, koji ih je stvorio, ako želite da se samo Njemu, jedinom, klanjate." (41:37)

Do not prostrate to the sun or the moon, but prostrate to Allah who created them, if it is Him alone you worship.

When he finished, Utba went back to his people and said:

"Čuo sam govor koji, tako mi Boga, nikada u životu ranije nisam čuo. Nije to ni pjesma, ni mađija, niti liturgija. Kurejševići! Poslušajte me i nemojte Muhammeda ometati u poslu koji čini. Tako mi Boga, iz riječi koje sam saslušao predviđam da će se krupne stvari dogoditi."

I heard a speech that, by God, I have never heard before in my life. It is neither poetry, nor magic, nor liturgy. Qurayshites! Listen to me and do not obstruct Muhammad in what he is doing. By God, from the words I have heard, I predict that great things will happen.

He was one of the men who wanted to kill Muhammad. And even he couldn't deny what he heard.

## The Threat Against Him

His own uncle, Abu Talib, through whom the Quraysh pressured him, gave him an ultimatum: stop preaching or face consequences.

Muhammad's answer is one of the most powerful moments recorded in his life. The book records his exact words:

"Striče! Tako mi Boga, kada bi mi stavili Sunce u desnu, a Mjesec u lijevu ruku, pod uslovom da ovo napustim, ne bih to učinio sve dotle dok se ne utvrdi istina o Allahu ili dok se na tom putu ne žrtvujem."

Uncle! By God, even if they placed the Sun in my right hand and the Moon in my left hand, on the condition that I abandon this, I would not do it until the truth about Allah is established or I am sacrificed on this path.

Sun and moon. The most powerful symbols in his culture. The ultimate temptation. He wouldn't trade his message for either.

## How He Treated His Enemies

When Muhammad conquered Mecca, the men who tortured his followers, who killed his companions, who plotted to assassinate him — they were at his mercy. They expected massacre. They expected revenge.

What they got instead was forgiveness.

The Prophet gathered them and said:

"Niko vas neće kazniti. Idite, slobodni ste."

No one will punish you. Go, you are free.

Not selective forgiveness. Total. The people who burned his friends, who stoned him, who threw dust on his head while he prayed — he forgave them.

That's character. That's what happens when someone is completely certain of their truth and completely confident in their position.

## His Personal Life

He married Khadijah when he was 25. She was 40, wealthy, and widowed. She knew his character from years of working with him. She was the first person to believe in his message. They remained married until she died — a 25-year marriage in which he never married anyone else.

After she died, his marriages served purposes: protecting widows, forming alliances, teaching women their rights in a society that treated them as property.

The Qur'an describes him:

"Jer ti si, zaista, najljepše ćudi" (68:4) — for indeed, you are of the highest character.

Allah didn't call him powerful. He didn't call him brilliant. He called him someone of the highest character.

## What Islam Says He Is

Islam doesn't say Muhammad is divine. It says he's the most perfect example of what a human can become.

The book explains: "Allah je oslobodio čovjeka od obožavanja idola, i mnogoboštva uopće, ali ga je istovremeno oslobodio i izrabljivanja i eksploatacije od strane drugog čovjeka. U tom smislu islam je ozakonio norme i Božiji poslanik Muhammed a.s. je praktičnim životom, riječju i djelom, dao primjer i obrazac kako treba razvijati islamsko društvo."

Allah freed man from idol worship and polytheism in general, but simultaneously freed him from exploitation and exploitation by other humans. In this sense, Islam codified norms and the Messenger of Allah Muhammad, through practical life, word, and deed, gave the example and model of how Islamic society should be developed.

He's not the message. He's the living example of it.

## His Final Instructions

On his deathbed, Muhammad's last words to his people were not about power or politics. They were about the people he loved most:

"Hvalite žene, jer vas Allah stvorio od njih, a žene su vaše pomoćnice."

Praise women, for Allah created you from them, and women are your partners.

Not "obey them." Not "control them." Praise them. Thank them.

## The Standard He Set

He was a man who:
- Never lied, even when lying would have saved his life
- Forgave the people who tortured him
- Treated slaves as equals
- Applied the law equally to his own daughter
- Refused power, wealth, and position offered directly to him
- Spent his life serving the poor and oppressed
- Died poor, with nothing

And Allah says about him:

"Mi smo te poslali svim ljudima da radosne vijesti donosiš i da opominješ" (34:28)

We sent you to all people to bring good tidings and to warn.

Not just for Arabs. Not just for Muslims. For all people.
