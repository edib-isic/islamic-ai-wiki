# Pillar 2 — AI for Halal Income

AI is one of the most powerful tools for halal income in this generation. It democratizes skills that used to require teams — now one person with AI can deliver what used to take ten.

## The Framework

### 1. Halal Revenue First

Every AI business must pass the test:
- No Riba-based structures ([see Riba](concepts/riba.md))
- No impermissible products ([AAOIFI principles](concepts/aaoifi-screening.md))
- Transparent pricing, no deception
- Real value delivered (not empty promises)

### 2. Skills → Services → Business

The progression:

**Step 1: Learn AI Skills (Weeks 1-4)**
- Prompt engineering for LLMs
- AI-powered content creation
- Automation with AI tools
- Basic AI application development

**Step 2: Offer Services (Weeks 5-12)**
- Freelancing on Upwork/Fiverr
- Direct outreach to Muslim businesses
- Portfolio building through real clients

**Step 3: Build Business (Months 4-12)**
- Productized services
- AI agency model
- Team and subcontractors

**Step 4: Scale (Year 2+)**
- Multiple revenue streams
- Passive income products
- Reinvest in Sadaqah Jariyah

### 3. AI Business Models (Ranked by Speed to Income)

| Model | Setup Time | Income Potential | Difficulty |
|-------|------------|------------------|------------|
| AI Freelancing | 1-2 weeks | €1-5K/month | Low |
| AI Consulting | 2-4 weeks | €3-10K/month | Medium |
| AI Agency | 2-3 months | €5-20K/month | Medium-High |
| AI Products | 3-6 months | Unbounded | High |
