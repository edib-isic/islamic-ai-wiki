# Income Model for Sadaqah Jariyah Wiki

**Sources:** Initial strategy design — August 15, 2026

## The Vision

This wiki is not just educational content. It is designed to **generate income** that funds ongoing charity — Sadaqah Jariyah. The model is self-sustaining: the knowledge base grows, revenue grows, charity grows.

## Revenue Streams (Tiered Approach)

### Tier 1: Free Access (The Core Wiki)

Everything in this wiki is free. This is the foundation of the Sadaqah Jariyah — knowledge that anyone can access and benefit from.

### Tier 2: Community & Coaching (Low-Cost, High-Value)

- **Monthly membership** — $5-15/month for:
  - Deeper content not in the public wiki
  - Weekly Q&A sessions
  - Accountability group for halal investing goals
  - Templates and frameworks
- **1-on-1 coaching** — $50-100/session:
  - Riba elimination roadmap
  - AI business strategy
  - Portfolio alignment with Islamic principles

### Tier 3: AI Services (Scalable, High-Income)

Use your software engineering + AI skills to offer services:

- **AI Agency** — Build AI solutions for businesses
  - Content automation for Muslim businesses
  - AI-powered Islamic education tools
  - Halal fintech integrations
- **Freelancing** — $50-150/hour on platforms
  - Custom AI tools
  - Automation scripts
  - Web development with AI integration
- **Consulting** — Help Muslim businesses adopt AI ethically

### Tier 4: Digital Products (Passive Income)

- **Courses** — Video courses based on wiki content
  - "Islamic Finance for Beginners"
  - "AI Skills for Muslim Professionals"
  - "Building a Halal AI Business"
- **E-books** — Deep dives on specific topics
- **Templates** — Notion templates, spreadsheets, checklists

### Tier 5: Sponsorships & Partnerships

- **Halal companies** sponsor sections of the wiki
- **Islamic finance platforms** pay for featured placement
- **Community-supported** — direct donations from beneficiaries

## Priority: AI Services First

Based on your profile (Software Engineer, late 40s, income under €50K, $45K debt), the fastest path to income is **AI freelancing and consulting**:

1. **Start freelancing now** — Offer AI automation services on Upwork/Fiverr
2. **Build portfolio** — Create 2-3 case studies
3. **Scale to agency** — Hire subcontractors, handle bigger projects
4. **Reinvest in wiki** — Use income to fund Sadaqah Jariyah projects

**Estimated timeline:**
- Month 1-2: Freelancing setup, first clients
- Month 3-6: Consistent income €2-5K/month
- Month 6-12: Agency scale, €5-10K/month
- Year 2+: Reinvest 20-50% in Sadaqah Jariyah projects

## Alignment with Islamic Principles

- **Halal revenue only** — No interest-based business structures, no haram products
- **Transparent pricing** — Clear value exchange, no deception
- **Teach others** — Every business interaction is an opportunity to share Islamic principles
- **Give generously** — Allocate portion of income to charity from day one
