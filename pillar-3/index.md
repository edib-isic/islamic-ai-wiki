# Pillar 3 — Teaching Frameworks

Knowledge that is not taught is wasted. This pillar contains ready-to-use lesson plans, course outlines, and teaching scripts so anyone can teach others — amplifying the Sadaqah Jariyah.

## The Philosophy

- **Modular** — Each lesson can stand alone or be part of a larger course
- **Cited** — All Islamic claims trace back to primary sources
- **Actionable** — Every lesson has practical takeaways
- **Repeatable** — Anyone can deliver these materials

- None yet. This is the next major build phase.

## Planned Modules

### Module 1: Islamic Finance Fundamentals (10 lessons)
- What is halal wealth?
- Why Riba is forbidden (deep dive)
- How to identify Riba in modern products
- Wealth purification step-by-step
- Islamic investing principles
- Zakat basics and calculation
- AAOIFI screening explained
- Common misconceptions about Islamic finance
- Building a halal portfolio
- Case studies: real Muslim investors

### Module 2: AI for Halal Income (8 lessons)
- AI basics for non-technical people
- How to use AI tools without haram
- Finding halal opportunities in AI
- Freelancing with AI skills ([roadmap](pillar-2/ai-freelancing-roadmap.md))
- Building an AI agency ([blueprint](pillar-2/ai-agency-blueprint.md))
- Pricing your AI services ([guide](pillar-2/ai-pricing-guide.md))
- Managing clients ethically
- Scaling from freelancer to business owner ([agency blueprint](pillar-2/ai-agency-blueprint.md))

### Module 3: Wealth as Amanah (5 lessons)
- Understanding Amanah in Islamic context
- The audit of wealth on Judgment Day
- Balancing dunya and akhirah
- Sadaqah Jariyah strategies
- Living as a steward, not an owner

## Format Options

Each module can be delivered as:
- **Video course** — recorded lessons with slides
- **Live workshop** — interactive sessions
- **Text guide** — written lessons in this wiki
- **Audio series** — podcast-style episodes
- **Printable PDF** — for distribution at masjids, events

## Who Can Teach

- Imams looking to add financial literacy to their curriculum
- Muslim entrepreneurs wanting to share knowledge
- Parents teaching their children about money
- Community organizers running workshops
- Anyone who believes in the mission
