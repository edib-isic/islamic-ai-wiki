# Pillar 1 — Faith & Knowing Allah

The most important knowledge is knowing Allah. Every other skill — AI, business, investing — is built on this foundation. Without it, success in this life can become destruction in the next.

## Core Principles

### 1. Everything is Amanah

Wealth, time, and knowledge are not owned by you — they are entrusted by Allah. You are a trustee, not an owner. You will be audited.

See: [Amanah — Sacred Trust](concepts/amanah.md)

### 2. Riba is Forbidden

Any guaranteed return detached from real economic activity is prohibited. Eliminating Riba is not optional — the Quran declares war from Allah and His Messenger on those who continue it.

See: [Riba — Prohibition of Interest](concepts/riba.md)

### 3. Trade is Permitted

> "Allah has permitted trade and has forbidden interest." [Quran 2:275.md](Quran 2:275.md)

Business, entrepreneurship, and earning through real value are honored in Islam. AI tools and skills are modern vehicles for this.

### 4. Knowledge is a Duty

Seeking knowledge is obligatory for every Muslim. Teaching others multiplies the reward. This wiki itself is an act of Sadaqah Jariyah.

## The Question Every Decision Must Answer

Before any business decision, investment choice, or career move:

**"Would this decision be acceptable on Judgment Day?"**

If the answer is unclear, the decision is unclear. Pause. Research. Ask a scholar.
