## Mission

Preparing Muslim investors to answer for their wealth on the Day of Judgment. Core framing: wealth is an Amanah (trust), every manager will be audited, the ultimate metric is the return on Akhirah — not return on investment.

## Tools

### Mission Pledge (themuslim-investor.com/pledge)

A written niyyah (intention) committing to:
- Akhirah as primary metric
- Eliminating Riba and Haram sources
- Converting knowledge to action (Amal)
- Viewing wealth as a tool for Khayr
- Sabr (patience) and Tawakkul (trust in Allah)

### IIRS — Islamic Investor Readiness Score (themuslim-investor.com/tools/iirs)

Assesses readiness across 4 pillars:
1. Riba Elimination (heaviest weight: 40 pts)
2. Emergency Fund (25 pts)
3. Expense Control (20 pts)
4. Savings Rate (15 pts)

Score of 62 = BUILDING status. Target: 75+ for "Investment Ready."

### Portfolio Mirror (themuslim-investor.com/tools/mirror)

Deep qualitative analysis of portfolio holdings against:
- Shariah compliance (AAOIFI standards)
- Diversification
- Risk alignment with investor profile
- Spiritual accountability framing

### Investor Profile (themuslim-investor.com/tools/profile)

Assesses 5 dimensions:
1. Spiritual Focus
2. Risk Tolerance
3. Knowledge Level
4. Time Horizon
5. Financial Stability

Outputs recommended allocation ranges based on profile.

## Compliance Framework

Uses **AAOIFI** (Accounting and Auditing Organization for Islamic Financial Institutions) standards for screening:
- Qualitative: core business must be permissible
- Quantitative: limits on interest-bearing debt, interest income, impermissible cash ratios

## Key Principles

- Riba prohibition (Quran 2:275) — any predetermined guaranteed return on money lent/invested detached from real economic activity
- Wealth as Amanah (Quran 57:7)
- Trade permitted, interest forbidden (Quran 2:275)
- Sadaqah Jariyah as primary wealth motivation
- AAOIFI screening as practical compliance tool
